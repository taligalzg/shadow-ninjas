Nobody asked her to do that.

A stranger asked Rivka for water.
She gave him some.
And then she offered water to his camels too.

Today we teach our kids "Don't talk to strangers."
And of course, safety comes first.

But Rivka teaches us something beautiful.
She noticed someone needed help.

That's what I want to teach my kids.
When you see a child sitting alone, invite them to play.

Because kindness starts with noticing others. 🧡

Shabbat Shalom.
